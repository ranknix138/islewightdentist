Your First Appointment

What to Expect During Your First Visit

A calm, comprehensive 30-minute appointment focused on listening, understanding your goals, and assessing your oral health.

📋

Medical & Dental History

We review your health history, current medications, any dental concerns, and past experiences to tailor your care safely.

🔍

Full Clinical Examination

A thorough check of your teeth, gums, bite alignment, jaw joints, and a soft tissue oral cancer screening.

📸

Low-Dose Digital X-Rays

High-definition digital radiographs allow us to inspect between teeth, beneath old fillings, and check underlying bone levels accurately.

📝

Clear Written Treatment Plan

If any treatment is required, we provide a transparent written estimate explaining all NHS and private options and exact costs.

Nervous & Phobic Patients

Haven't Seen a Dentist in Years? You're in Safe Hands.

Dental anxiety is very common. We never judge or lecture. Our clinicians pride themselves on gentle numbing techniques, clear communication, and working completely at your pace.
