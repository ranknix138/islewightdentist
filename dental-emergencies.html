Common Dental Emergencies We Treat

If you are experiencing any of the following symptoms, contact our Newport clinic without delay.

⚡

Severe Toothache

Constant throbbing or sharp pain that prevents sleep, eating, or normal activities often indicates nerve inflammation or infection.

💥

Broken, Cracked or Chipped Teeth

Trauma or biting into hard foods can fracture tooth structure, exposing sensitive dentine or sharp edges that cut the tongue.

🩹

Dental Abscess or Facial Swelling

Swollen gums or cheek accompanied by fever or a foul taste indicates a bacterial infection requiring prompt clinical drainage or antibiotics.

🦷

Knocked-Out (Avulsed) Adult Tooth

Time is critical! Handle the tooth only by the white crown, store it in cold milk or saliva, and reach us within 60 minutes for replantation.

👑

Lost Filling or Dislodged Crown

Exposed underlying teeth can be highly sensitive to hot and cold. We can place a soothing dressing or recement your crown.

🩸

Uncontrolled Post-Extraction Bleeding

If bleeding continues despite biting firmly on a clean rolled gauze for 20 minutes, call our surgery for immediate clinical assistance.

Immediate Steps

What to Do While You Wait to See Us

1
Take Suitable Over-the-Counter Pain Relief

Paracetamol or ibuprofen (if medically suitable) helps moderate inflammation. Never place an aspirin tablet directly against gums as it burns soft tissue.

2
Rinse with Warm Salt Water

Dissolve half a teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water. Gently rinse to cleanse the area and reduce bacterial load.

3
Apply a Cold Compress Externally

If your cheek or jaw is swollen, hold an ice pack wrapped in a cloth to the outside of your face for 10-minute intervals.

When to Visit A&E:

If swelling extends towards your eye or down your neck, or if you experience difficulty breathing, swallowing, or opening your mouth, go directly to St Mary's Hospital A&E or call 999.

Transparent NHS Emergency Fee

Emergency Treatment Cost

£26.80

Under the NHS, urgent dental care has a fixed statutory charge of £26.80. This covers clinical examination, diagnosis, X-rays, and immediate treatment to relieve pain (such as temporary dressings or emergency prescriptions). Patients with valid NHS exemption certificates receive this treatment free of charge.
