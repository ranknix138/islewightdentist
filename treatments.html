Foundation of Healthy Smiles

Routine Dental Examinations

Regular dental check-ups allow our clinical team to monitor your oral health, spot issues early, and prevent dental decay and gum disease before they cause pain.

What Happens During Your Check-Up?

Whether you attend every six months or haven't visited a dentist in years, our experienced clinicians provide thorough, gentle assessments in a supportive, relaxed environment.

  • ✓ Full Oral Examination: Detailed inspection of all teeth, existing restorations, and soft oral tissues.
  • ✓ Oral Cancer Screening: Routine visual checks of cheeks, tongue, palate, and gums.
  • ✓ Low-Dose Digital X-Rays: Safe radiography to check between teeth and assess root health.
  • ✓ Periodontal Gum Scoring: Evaluating gum health to detect any early signs of gingivitis.
  • ✓ Personalised Advice: Tailored guidance on diet, oral hygiene, and home care.

Protecting Gums & Breath

Dental Hygiene & Preventive Care

Prevent gum disease, remove hard calculus deposits, and keep your teeth feeling fresh and smooth with regular professional hygiene appointments.

Why Are Hygiene Visits Vital?

Gum disease is the primary cause of tooth loss in UK adults, yet it is often completely painless in its early stages. Regular professional scaling removes hardened plaque and tartar that normal brushing cannot dislodge.

  • ✓ Tartar & Plaque Removal: Ultrasonic and delicate hand scaling along the gumline.
  • ✓ Gingivitis Prevention: Stopping bleeding gums, swelling, and chronic inflammation.
  • ✓ Fresh Breath Support: Removing bacteria responsible for halitosis.
  • ✓ Preventive Fluoride Applications: Strengthening tooth enamel against acid attacks.

Dental Emergencies in Newport

Teeth Alignment & Bite Assessment

Orthodontic Consultations

Featured in our patient booking portal: comprehensive orthodontic consultations for children and adults to evaluate teeth alignment, crowding, spacing, and bite harmony.

What is an Orthodontic Consultation?

An orthodontic consultation is a dedicated clinical assessment where we examine your smile, discuss how your teeth meet when you chew, and explore modern straightening solutions tailored to your goals.

  • ✓ Alignment & Crowding Assessment: Evaluating crooked teeth, gaps, and overbites.
  • ✓ Children & Adult Evaluations: Assessing development in teenagers or adult realignment.
  • ✓ Clear Guidance: Discussing suitable orthodontic options, timelines, and costs.
  • ✓ Transparent Discussion: Clear, honest advice without any pressure.
