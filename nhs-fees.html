Free NHS Dental Care

Who is Exempt from Paying?

You do not have to pay for NHS dental treatment if, when the treatment starts, you meet any of the official NHS criteria below:

👶
Children & Young People

Anyone under 18 years of age, or under 19 and receiving qualifying full-time education.

🤰
Pregnant or New Mothers

Pregnant women or mothers who have had a baby in the last 12 months (valid MatEx certificate required).

🏛️
Qualifying Low Income Benefits

Income Support, Income-related ESA, Income-based JSA, Pension Credit guarantee credit, or Universal Credit (meeting earnings criteria).

📄
HC2 / HC3 Certificates

Holders of a valid NHS Low Income Scheme HC2 certificate (full help) or HC3 certificate (partial help).

* Please bring physical or digital proof of your exemption to your appointment so our team can verify your NHS claim correctly.
