Getting to Newport

Travel & Parking Information

Helpful directions for visiting Chapel House, Newport, Isle of Wight.

🚗

By Car & Parking

Chapel Street is easily accessible via the main Newport thoroughfares. Ample public pay-and-display car parks are situated nearby in central Newport within a 2-4 minute walk.

🚌

By Bus (Southern Vectis)

Newport Bus Station is the central transit hub of the Isle of Wight, with frequent routes arriving from Ryde, Cowes, Sandown, Shanklin, Yarmouth, and Ventnor. Our practice is a short stroll from the station.

♿

Accessibility

Our clinic features ground-floor access for pushchairs, walkers, and wheelchairs. If you have specific mobility requirements, please inform our reception team in advance.
